Figure S#. Effective dimensionality of population activity during the probe and goal periods.
Participation ratio of single-trial population activity for S1, S2, M1A, and M1B during the probe
(grey) and goal (blue) stimulus periods. Effective dimensionality was significantly lower during the
goal period in all four areas, consistent with task representations converging onto a lower-dimensional,
shared subspace during goal-directed behavior. Error bars: SEM. n = 40 sessions from 6 animals.
**P<0.01, ***P<0.001, paired Student's t test.
